Lead Generation

Insurance Litigation Group already runs real, active paid lead generation across Google and Meta for both First-Party Property and Immigration — but First-Party Property leads have declined, and the firm is virtually invisible in organic search against named local competitors with far larger review footprints. The result is a firm spending real money on ads while leaving significant, lower-cost visibility on the table.

Website Audit

The Problem
  • Attorney bios are not visible on the homepage itself — the site references an "Our Team" link, but no bios rendered in this research pass
  • PageSpeed scores could not be independently verified this session — public API access was blocked on repeated attempts (HTTP 429); recommend a manual mobile and desktop PageSpeed check before the proposal call
  • No video content was found anywhere on the site — no attorney introduction, no case-result videos
  • The firm's own FindLaw profile lists a second live domain (restorationlaw.com) for the same firm name, address, and phone — splitting SEO authority across two websites instead of consolidating it under ilgpa.com
Blind Spot: You may not realize your firm is effectively running two competing websites — ilgpa.com and restorationlaw.com both serve live content under the Insurance Litigation Group brand, which means every backlink and every bit of Google trust you've built is being split in half instead of compounding on one site.
The Positive
  • Clear, high-visibility call-to-action above the fold — two prominently displayed "Damage Hotline" phone numbers
  • Contact form is present above the fold in the main content area, not buried on a separate page
The Gap
  • Missing attorney bios and video remove the trust signals that matter most to a first-time caller deciding whether to trust a firm with their claim
  • Unverified site performance means the true cost of every dollar of paid traffic pointed at this site can't yet be measured
  • Splitting authority across two live domains means neither one ranks as well as a single, consolidated site would
A site that converts every dollar of the traffic you're already paying for is what makes scaling ad spend across 6 offices actually pay off — not just generate more clicks.

Owning local search in Miami is what turns 45 employees and 6 offices into the dominant, unmissable First-Party Property and Immigration firm in South Florida — instead of one more name buried below better-optimized competitors.
