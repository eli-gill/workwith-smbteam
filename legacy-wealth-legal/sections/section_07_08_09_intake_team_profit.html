Intake Assessment

Roughly 100 daily emails from existing casework are triaged by Martin personally, one at a time, with no process behind them. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: RED
The Problem
  • You personally handle every intake interaction — email triage, document generation, and client communication — with no delegation in place
  • Roughly 100 emails come in daily from existing case matters, all needing personal review, with no system to triage or prioritize them
  • No CRM, intake tracking, or defined follow-up sequence exists for new estate planning inquiries
  • No after-hours or weekend coverage — every prospective client depends entirely on you personally being available
Blind Spot: Automating the busywork with AI is the right instinct, but a faster inbox is not the same as an intake process — without one, growth in case volume will just multiply the email burden you are trying to escape.
The Gap
  • Every day spent personally triaging ~100 emails is a day not spent growing the new estate planning practice or meeting new prospects
  • Without a defined intake process, even a faster inbox won't guarantee warm estate planning prospects are followed up on quickly enough to convert
  • A single point of failure exists in every client relationship — if you are unavailable, nothing moves
Automating the busywork is the right first step — but real freedom only comes when an intake system, not just a faster inbox, is running the business for you.

Team Assessment

Legacy Wealth Legal is, by Martin's own description, a one-man operation. Until this changes, the owner cannot have more free time — no matter how much revenue grows.

Status: RED
"Chief Everything Officer"
The Problem
  • Zero employees — every function (intake, drafting, marketing, billing) runs through Martin personally
  • Explicitly hesitant to hire, weighing a "$1k/mo AI" against a "$50k/yr salary" rather than building a team
  • No org structure, delegation, or accountability system of any kind exists yet
  • Growth plans toward $500k–$1M currently assume Martin continues doing everything himself
Blind Spot: Choosing AI over a first hire solves the busywork, but it does not solve the fact that a firm entirely dependent on one person cannot scale past what one person can personally do in a day — no matter how good the AI is.
The Gap
  • As case volume grows toward the $500k–$1M goal, so does Martin's personal workload — with no team, there is no ceiling on his time and no path to stepping back
  • Without at least one support hire, AI-driven programs built for firms with staff are not available to Legacy Wealth Legal yet, delaying the automation goal Martin raised on the call
  • Every new client relationship is currently a single point of failure — if Martin is unavailable, nothing moves
The "firm that runs itself" Martin is describing cannot exist with a team of one — freedom requires eventually building the very team he is currently trying to avoid.

Profit Plan Assessment

Legacy Wealth Legal has no stated revenue baseline yet — Martin has a $500k–$1M growth goal but no current gross or net figure, fee structure, or average case value on record. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

Status: RED
The Problem
  • No current annual revenue figure — gross or net — has been established for the new estate planning practice
  • No average case value or fee structure has been defined for estate planning matters
  • No close rate has been tracked (defaulting to a 15% industry baseline for planning purposes)
  • Financial decisions are being made by comparing costs to a single salary figure, not a profit plan
Blind Spot: Comparing every expense to a single salary line is a useful gut check early on, but it is not a profit plan — without knowing what an estate planning case is actually worth once it closes, it's impossible to know whether $1k/mo, $10k/mo, or $50k/yr is the right number to spend.
The Gap
  • Without a baseline revenue or case-value figure, there is no way to measure whether the $500k–$1M goal is being approached or missed in real time
  • No documented margin means every new cost decision (AI, staff, marketing) is being made on instinct rather than data
  • A firm that cannot yet answer "what is a client worth?" cannot reliably know which growth investments will pay for themselves
Real freedom is not just a firm that runs itself — it's a firm whose owner knows exactly what it's worth, and Legacy Wealth Legal isn't there yet.
