Here Is Where Legacy Wealth Legal Stands Today

Martin Birenbaum spent 34 years building a business-law practice before launching Legacy Wealth Legal, a brand-new estate planning firm in Palmetto Bay, FL. He is running it entirely alone — intake, drafting, and roughly 100 daily case emails all pass through him personally — with no live website content, no Google Business Profile, and no team in place. The single most important finding: this is a firm at day one of its public existence, with real casework already coming in but none of the systems needed to support it at scale.

Stage 6 is the stage where Legacy Wealth Legal runs without needing Martin to personally answer every email — and it is exactly where we are taking him.

Where You Are Today

Stage 3: Solo Practitioner
  • Martin personally handles every case, every email, and every prospective client — there is no delegation of any kind
  • The estate planning brand has no live website content, no Google Business Profile, and no reviews
  • Roughly 100 daily emails from existing casework are triaged one at a time, with no system behind them
  • Explicitly avoiding a first hire, comparing every cost decision to a $50k/yr salary instead of building a team

Where You Need To Be

Stage 6: Law Firm Owner
  • The firm runs without you
  • Your income is no longer tied to your personal hours
  • Your time is yours to spend however you choose
  • This is where true freedom and true wealth become possible
The gap between where Legacy Wealth Legal is today and a firm that runs itself is not about working harder — it is about putting a process and eventually a small team around the work Martin is already doing alone.

The Law Firm Growth Acceleration Model

We Help Law Firm Owners Get More Profit, More Personal Freedom, And Outstanding Results For Their Clients.

Most law firms try to grow by chasing more leads. But leads alone are not the problem — and leads alone are not the answer. Here is what actually works.

Lead Generation

Leads are to a law firm what gasoline is to a car. Without them, you go nowhere. But a steady, predictable flow of leads changes everything — you get to choose your cases instead of taking whatever comes in.

Intake

But all the leads in the world are worthless without a system to turn them into paying clients. Most firms are losing cases they already paid for simply because their follow-up is slow or inconsistent.

Self-Managing Team

Once clients are coming in, you need a team — otherwise you end up working 90-hour weeks and results suffer. A self-managing team is what gives you the freedom to step back.

Profit Plan

And once the team is in place, most owners look at their bank account and ask: where did all the money go? That is what a profit plan solves — clarity on what you earn, what you keep, and how to grow both.

All four have to work together. If one is missing, you cannot have more profit, more freedom, and outstanding results for your clients at the same time.

Intake Assessment

Roughly 100 daily emails from existing casework are triaged by Martin personally, one at a time, with no process behind them. Fixing intake means converting more of the leads the firm is already generating. This is where the biggest immediate ROI lives.

Status: RED
The Problem
  • You personally handle every intake interaction — email triage, document generation, and client communication — with no delegation in place
  • Roughly 100 emails come in daily from existing case matters, all needing personal review, with no system to triage or prioritize them
  • No CRM, intake tracking, or defined follow-up sequence exists for new estate planning inquiries
  • No after-hours or weekend coverage — every prospective client depends entirely on you personally being available
Blind Spot: Automating the busywork with AI is the right instinct, but a faster inbox is not the same as an intake process — without one, growth in case volume will just multiply the email burden you are trying to escape.
The Gap
  • Every day spent personally triaging ~100 emails is a day not spent growing the new estate planning practice or meeting new prospects
  • Without a defined intake process, even a faster inbox won't guarantee warm estate planning prospects are followed up on quickly enough to convert
  • A single point of failure exists in every client relationship — if you are unavailable, nothing moves
Automating the busywork is the right first step — but real freedom only comes when an intake system, not just a faster inbox, is running the business for you.

Legacy Wealth Legal has no stated revenue baseline yet — Martin has a $500k–$1M growth goal but no current gross or net figure, fee structure, or average case value on record. Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity. Until there is a clear profit plan, growth may just mean more work without more wealth.

The Problem
  • No current annual revenue figure — gross or net — has been established for the new estate planning practice
  • No average case value or fee structure has been defined for estate planning matters
  • No close rate has been tracked (defaulting to a 15% industry baseline for planning purposes)

Blind Spot: Comparing every expense to a single salary line is a useful gut check early on, but it is not a profit plan — without knowing what an estate planning case is actually worth once it closes, it's impossible to know whether $1k/mo, $10k/mo, or $50k/yr is the right number to spend.
